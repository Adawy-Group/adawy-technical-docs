# `aladawy-shop`

<Badge tone="client">Client</Badge>

**Client project.** The storefront for **Aladawy Shop**, a packaging,
disposables and kitchen-supply house serving restaurants, bakeries, groceries
and households in Egypt.

Private · standalone Next.js 16 app · not part of the monorepo · live at
`aladawy-shop.vercel.app`

Bilingual Arabic/English with real RTL, **Arabic-default** (`/` redirects to
`/ar`, not `/en`). 53 catalogue lines across 6 categories. No database, no
account, no payment: the catalogue is committed TypeScript and the checkout is
a **quote request** ([ADR-005](/architecture/adrs)).

## The domain fact that shapes the whole codebase

**Every line has two prices, and they do not always disagree.** The counter's
price list states سعر القطاعي (retail) and سعر الجملة (wholesale) per unit in
EGP, and `lib/content/catalog.ts` mirrors it as `pricing: { retail,
wholesale? }`. The customer picks a tier and a quantity, and the running figure
is an **exact total**. What the counter settles before anything ships is
*stock*, not price.

The two columns agree more often than they differ. Of the 53 lines, 19 carry a
real wholesale discount, 24 state the same figure twice, and 10 leave
wholesale blank — so the interface says which of three situations a line is in
rather than always showing a pair:

```
Glue Sticks · code 384 · sold by the pack · min 10, in steps of 5
  Retail       160 EGP / pack        ← a real choice
  Wholesale    120 EGP / pack

Lemon Squeezer · code 149 · sold by the piece
  45 EGP / piece — one price, retail and wholesale

Large Strainer · code 397 · sold by the piece
  60 EGP / piece — wholesale price on request
```

The rules that follow from it:

1. **`pricingShape()` in `lib/content/catalog.ts` is the only place the rule
   lives.** It returns `choice` (wholesale is lower), `single` (the two agree)
   or `retail-only` (wholesale blank). The product page, the card, the filter
   facet and the rehydration guard all read it. *Why:* a radio group offering
   two identical figures, or one with an empty side, offers a choice that does
   not exist, and a price list that does that stops being believed.
2. **An absent `wholesale` is not the same as one equal to `retail`.** Blank
   means the figure has to be asked for (`wholesaleOnRequest`); equal means
   there is nothing to ask (`onePriceNote`). Do not collapse them into one
   field.
3. **Use `formatPrice`, not `formatNumber`, for money.** `formatNumber` pins
   `maximumFractionDigits: 0` because quantities are whole; سلك مواعير is 5.75
   at wholesale against 6 at retail, and rounding would print the wholesale
   price as the higher of the two.
4. **`lib/store/quote-slice.ts` is a quote, not a cart.** Tier **and** option
   are part of a line's identity (`slug::tier::option`), because the same tape
   at retail and at wholesale are two different things to quote.
5. **Stock is deliberately not in the catalogue.** A committed TypeScript file
   goes stale within a week, and telling someone an item is out of stock when
   the counter has it is worse than saying nothing.
6. **Payment is absent by design, not by omission.** The business does not
   take online payment today. The quote slice already carries everything an
   order needs, priced, and `app/api/quote/route.ts` already validates the full
   payload server-side, so a checkout can arrive without a rewrite.

The catalogue test enforces the shape: a positive retail price on every line,
wholesale never above retail, and a tier choice offered only where wholesale is
genuinely lower.

This is the only repo in the org using **Redux Toolkit** — justified by a
quote that persists across routes and reloads, which is genuinely
cross-cutting client state. Do not take it as a house pattern.

**TypeScript 7 runs side by side with 6.** `@typescript/native` (TS 7)
provides the `tsc` binary, while `typescript` is aliased to
`@typescript/typescript6` because typescript-eslint and Next still load the TS
6 API. **ESLint 10** wraps `eslint-config-next`'s configs in `@eslint/compat`'s
`fixupConfigRules`, because its react, jsx-a11y and import plugins still call
context APIs ESLint 10 removed — drop the wrapper once they catch up.

## Content lives in two trees, and they must stay in sync

`lib/content/catalog.ts` holds **only what does not translate** — slugs,
codes, categories, units, minimums, steps, prices. Every human-readable string
lives in `messages/{en,ar}.json` under `Products.items.<slug>`, keyed by the
same slug. Adding a product is one catalogue entry plus one block in **both**
message files. This is the same pattern as
[`aladawy-pack`](/repositories/aladawy-pack) — see
[i18n & RTL](/standards/i18n-rtl) for the rule and the reasoning.

`messages/parity.test.ts` is the gate, in three groups:

- **Locale parity** — same keys in both locales, no empty strings, the same
  ICU arguments in both, and Arabic plurals carrying the `two`/`few`
  categories the language actually has.
- **Catalogue coverage** — every product named in both locales, no orphaned
  message blocks, every declared option translated, no option label without a
  catalogue option.
- **Catalogue integrity** — unique slugs and codes, codes printed exactly as
  the price list prints them, the pricing rules above, and every minimum a
  whole number of steps.

*Why a test and not a review step:* `next-intl` fails at render time, so a
drifted key would otherwise surface as a broken prerendered page.

## i18n and RTL

- Middleware is **`proxy.ts` at the repo root**, not `middleware.ts` — Next.js
  16 renamed the entry point.
- **Import `Link`, `redirect`, `usePathname`, `useRouter` from
  `@/i18n/navigation`**, never from `next/link` or `next/navigation`. *Why:*
  the raw ones drop the locale prefix and 404. `useSearchParams` is the
  exception — a query string carries no locale.
- Every page calls `setRequestLocale(locale)` before `getTranslations`. *Why:*
  that is what keeps the route statically prerendered.
- `getDirection()` in `i18n/routing.ts` is the only place `rtl` is decided.
- Use logical utilities (`ps-`/`pe-`, `ms-`/`me-`, `start-`/`end-`), never
  `pl-`/`left-`. Arabic is the default, so physical sides are wrong by default.

Four RTL traps already hit:

- **Arabic is never tracked.** Letter-spacing severs cursive joins, so
  `:lang(ar)` zeroes every `--tracking-*` variable plus `.eyebrow` and the
  display sizes. Latin inside an Arabic page keeps its tracking by carrying its
  own `lang`.
- **Numeric ranges reverse.** An en dash is bidi-neutral, so `34–48` inside
  Arabic renders max-first. `formatRange()` wraps the expression in an LTR
  isolate; its one remaining call site is the shop's price-filter chip. A lone
  price has no separator to mis-order, which is why `formatPrice()` needs no
  isolate.
- **`Sheet` takes physical sides.** `side="right"` is the *start* edge in
  Arabic. Use `useInlineSide()` from `lib/use-inline-side.ts`.
- **Arabic needs its own leading.** The display scale runs at 0.86
  line-height, which clips shaddas. The `:lang(ar)` overrides at the bottom of
  `globals.css` are deliberately *unlayered*, because unlayered CSS outranks
  Tailwind's `@layer utilities`.

Numerals stay Latin in both locales (`ar-EG-u-nu-latn`). *Why:* codes and
prices are read against a printed counter list that uses Latin digits.

## Brand, colour and type

The 2025 identity is four colours — `#000000`, `#1d1d1d`, `#c0272d`, `#ffffff`
— and the product photography carries the rest. Sections land on
`.surface-paper` (white), `.surface-field` (black) or `.surface-brand`
(`#c0272d`), and all three re-declare the same slots (`--ink`, `--ink-muted`,
`--line`, `--surface`) so a component works on any of them without knowing
which.

- **Anything that must invert per surface belongs in `@theme inline`**, not
  plain `@theme`, which resolves a `var()` once into `:root` and freezes it.
  That block also aliases every shadcn colour onto these slots, which is why
  unmodified shadcn primitives invert correctly.
- **`--signal` is live interface state** (selected tier, active filter, quote
  count, primary button): brand red on paper and field, white on a brand
  surface so the CTA does not vanish. `--brand` is the identity red and never
  inverts. `primary` points at `--signal`, not `--ink`.
- **The greys are contrast-bound, not taste-bound.** `--ink-faint` (eyebrows,
  codes, hints) must clear WCAG AA 4.5:1; `--line` is a 1.35:1 decorative
  hairline and must never border a form control — `--line-control` exists for
  that. Verify any change with a contrast calculation, not by eye.
- Radii are zeroed globally except `rounded-full`.

Type: Latin display is Inter (stand-in for Helvetica Now Display, `opsz`
axis), Latin body Montserrat; Arabic display Tajawal, Arabic body Cairo; codes
Geist Mono in both locales. Never put a Latin display face on an Arabic
heading — the swap lives in `app/fonts.ts` and the `:lang(ar)` cascade.

## Features worth knowing about before you change something

- **Catalogue filtering lives in the URL** —
  `?category=&wholesale=&unit=&tag=&min=&max=&q=&sort=`, parsed by
  `lib/shop/filters.ts` and applied on the **server**, so a filtered view is
  shareable and arrives in the HTML. Redux holds only what the URL cannot
  (quote, saved lines, recently viewed, overlay state); the two never mirror
  each other, so there is no sync to get wrong.
- **Search folds Arabic orthography** — hamza, tāʾ marbūṭa, alef maqṣūra,
  tatweel and diacritics, so `معلقه` finds `معلقة` — and matches item codes as
  well as names, via a ⌘K palette and an inline field. Leading zeros fold too,
  so an old `?q=0059` still resolves to `59`.
- **`/p/<code>` looks up a رقم الصنف read down the phone** and redirects to the
  canonical product URL with a **307**, not 308. *Why:* a code is the one
  identifier a supplier can reassign, which is also why the canonical URL is the
  slug. An unknown code falls through to `/shop?q=<code>` rather than a 404,
  because the digit was probably mistyped. It lives under `[locale]` so
  `proxy.ts` resolves `/p/384` to `/ar/p/384` without a second locale rule.
- **Quote and wishlist persist to localStorage**, versioned, and are
  **re-validated against the catalogue on read** (tier included, via
  `tiersOf()`), so a retired product or a changed price shape can never strand
  a line.

## Forms and enquiries

Each form has a `lib/schemas/*.ts` zod schema shared by the client and its API
route, which re-validates. Error messages are i18n keys in the `Errors`
namespace.

- **The resolver only sees form state.** `quoteContactSchema` excludes
  `lines`, which live in the store; the route uses `quoteRequestSchema`, which
  adds them back. *Why:* validating `lines` in the resolver fails on a field
  with no input, and the form silently refuses to submit.
- **Use `Controller` for `Select`**, not `watch()` + `setValue`. *Why:*
  `watch()` returns a function the React Compiler refuses to memoise, which
  opts the whole component out of compilation.
- **The honeypot parses when filled**, so the route answers `200` and drops the
  message rather than returning `422` and teaching the bot which field caught
  it.
- **Egyptian mobiles** are accepted in national (`01012345678`) and
  `+20`/`0020`/bare-`20` forms, normalised to one stored shape.
- **Both routes are rate-limited** by `lib/rate-limit.ts`: 6 requests per IP
  per minute, in memory and therefore per instance. *Why:* it stops the naive
  flood these forms attract without pulling Redis into a site with no runtime
  state; that function is the one place to swap for a real limiter.

`lib/enquiry.ts` is the single delivery seam — today a structured
`console.info`, and wiring a provider is one function body
([forms & enquiries](/guides/forms-and-enquiries)). It is deliberately not
half-wired: a misconfigured mail transport fails silently in production.

## Tests

Vitest 5, `**/*.test.ts`, `@/` aliased in `vitest.config.ts`. Two files:

- `messages/parity.test.ts` — locale parity, catalogue coverage and catalogue
  integrity (above).
- `lib/schemas/quote-request.test.ts` — every accepted phone shape and its
  normalisation, at least one line, tier must be a real tier id, whole positive
  quantities, an empty email input accepted but a malformed one rejected, a
  required governorate, and a filled honeypot that still parses.

## CI and deploy

**There is no CI.** The repo has no `.github/workflows`, so nothing runs the
tests, lint or typecheck on a push — the gates are yours to run by hand before
you merge. That is a gap, not a design
([the matrix](/repositories#what-the-repos-actually-share)). The repo also
records no deploy mechanism; production is `aladawy-shop.vercel.app`.

`.gitattributes` pins checkouts to LF. *Why:* Prettier enforces LF, so a
Windows checkout with `core.autocrlf=true` would fail `format:check` on every
file.

## SEO and going live

The client's domain, `aladawyshop.com`, still serves the old site, and its DNS
and email live on the group's Verpex host. When it moves
([the procedure](/guides/move-a-domain-to-vercel)), `PRODUCTION_HOST` changes
from the Vercel alias to the real domain, in both files.

**`PRODUCTION_HOST` is set** — to `aladawy-shop.vercel.app` in both
`lib/site.ts` and `next.config.ts` (restated there because Next loads the
config outside the app bundle, where `@/` does not resolve). The two must
match. *Why it was set on the Vercel alias:* with it `null`, every absolute URL
(og:image, canonical, JSON-LD) pointed at the per-deployment hostname, which
sits behind Vercel deployment protection, so scrapers got an SSO redirect
instead of the share card. When the shop gets its own domain, change both
files together ([Client Projects](/how-we-work/client-projects)).

On the production host, `X-Robots-Tag` is stated positively:
`index, follow, max-image-preview:large, max-snippet:-1` for pages, `noindex,
follow` for `/<locale>/quote` and `/<locale>/wishlist` (their contents live in
localStorage, so crawlers see an empty shell), and `noindex, nofollow` for
`/api/*`. Preview and branch deploys send `noindex, nofollow` everywhere.

- Inner pages get metadata from `lib/seo/page-metadata.ts`, which sets
  `openGraph` and `twitter` explicitly. *Why:* returning either object
  replaces the inherited one rather than merging, and would drop the share
  card.
- `jsonLdScript()` escapes `<` so no catalogue field can close the script tag.
  A product emits `Offer` with a real `price`, or `AggregateOffer` bounded by
  its two figures when `pricingShape()` says `choice`.
- **One share card for the whole site**, `public/og.png` (1200×630, PNG, no
  text), with dimensions stated only in `ogImage()`. It is a PNG because
  scrapers that decline AVIF/WebP leave the preview blank; products share it
  because a 1:1 photo gets centre-cropped. `pnpm og:bake` cuts it from
  `app/icon.png` so the card and favicon are the same black.

## Assets

`pnpm assets:bake` reads originals from outside the repo
(`ALADAWY_SOURCE_DIR`, keyed by WooCommerce post id) and writes AVIF/WebP pairs
into `public/products/` (`<slug>.avif` / `<slug>.webp`). Output is committed, so a fresh clone builds without the sources. The bake uses
`fit: cover` with attention cropping so each photo's own grey sweep fills the
card. **Do not inset the image in the card** — that reintroduces the letterbox
the bake exists to remove.
