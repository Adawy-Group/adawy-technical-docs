## The two worlds

**Group work** is Adawy Group's own brands — Adawy Group, Adawy Studio, Adawy
Pack, and the rest of the portfolio. Every brand **site** lives in one
monorepo, `adawy-platform`, on shared `@adawy/*` packages. This is where
[ADR-001](/architecture/adrs) and [ADR-008](/architecture/adrs) apply, and it is
the only place they apply.

**Client work** is software the department builds *for other businesses*.
Aladawy Pack (its website and its link page), Aladawy Shop, Jumeira Catering,
Zad Al-Hkoul and Baron are customers of the department, not Group brands. Each gets **its own standalone
repository**, deliberately not coupled to `@adawy/*` — see
[ADR-010](/architecture/adrs) for why, and
[Client Projects](/how-we-work/client-projects) for how to run one.

Both worlds share the department's *standards* — TypeScript strict, bilingual
and RTL from day one, accessibility, the same review and deployment
discipline. What they do not share is **code**.

### The third case, and the open question

[`adawy-group-links`](/repositories/adawy-group-links) is Adawy Group's own
property in a standalone repository, which is neither pattern. It is a
one-screen microsite rather than a brand site, and the repository itself
records no reason for sitting outside the monorepo. Its twin,
[`aladawy-links`](/repositories/aladawy-links), is *not* part of the question:
it belongs to a client, so a standalone repo is exactly what ADR-010 asks for.

Until that is decided, read the rule as stated above — **Group brand *sites*
live in the monorepo** — and treat the microsite as a single open case, not a
precedent. Starting a new Group property outside the platform is still an
[ADR-010](/architecture/adrs) decision, not a default.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Adawy Pack and Aladawy Pack are two different companies**, one letter
  apart.

  - **Adawy** Pack is a Group brand. It lives at `apps/pack` inside
    `adawy-platform`.
  - **Alad**awy Pack (Aladawy Pack for Printing & Packaging, est. 1971) is a
    client. Its website lives in `aladawy-pack`, its link-in-bio page in
    `aladawy-links`.

  They have different owners, different domains, different codebases, and
  different rules. When someone says "the pack site", ask which one.
</Callout>

If you are new, clone [`adawy-technical-docs`](/repositories/adawy-technical-docs)
first. It is the smallest repo, it builds in under a minute, and getting it
running proves your toolchain before anything harder depends on it.

## What the repos actually share

Moving between repos is cheap because they agree on most things. They do not
agree on all of them, and a rule you assume is universal is worse than one you
know is not — so here is the honest matrix.

| | Package manager | AGENTS.md | CI | Tests | Bilingual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `adawy-platform` | pnpm 11.26 | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | en · ar · fr |
| `adawy-group-links` | pnpm 11.26 | ⚠️ stub | ⚠️ deploy skipped | ✅ | en · ar |
| `aladawy-links` | pnpm 11.26 | ⚠️ stub | ❌ | ✅ | en · ar |
| `aladawy-pack` | pnpm 11.26 | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | en · ar |
| `aladawy-shop` | pnpm 11.26 | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ar · en |
| `jumeira` | pnpm 11.26 | ✅ +4 more | ✅ | ✅ | ar · en |
| `zad-alhkoul` | pnpm 11.26 | ⚠️ stub | ✅ | ✅ | en · ar |
| `baron-menu` | npm (image script only) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | en · ar |
| `adawy-technical-docs` | **npm** | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ | English only |

**What is genuinely universal:**

- **`main` is the deployable branch.** Work lands through reviewed pull
  requests ([git workflow](/how-we-work/git-workflow)).
- **A `README.md`** covering what the project is, how to run it, and what
  ships. The README is the tour; `AGENTS.md` is the contract.
- **Bilingual with real RTL** in every product repo — `baron-menu` included —
  with every human-readable string in a message file rather than in a component
  (`baron-menu`, having no framework, keeps them in one `I18N` table in
  `script.js` and the `*Ar` fields of `data/menu.js`)
  ([i18n & RTL](/standards/i18n-rtl)). This handbook is the exception, English
  only by decision ([ADR-007](/architecture/adrs)).
- **Prettier settles formatting.** Where CI exists it gates on it; formatting is
  never a review conversation.

**Where the gaps are, and what they mean:**

- **`aladawy-pack` and `baron-menu` have no `AGENTS.md`**, and in
  `adawy-group-links`, `aladawy-links` and `zad-alhkoul` the file holds only the
  block Next.js generates. `zad-alhkoul` and `aladawy-links` at least carry
  their constraints in a tracked README; `adawy-group-links` keeps them in an
  untracked local notes file. If
  you are the next person into one of those repos, writing it is the
  highest-value small PR
  available ([working with AI](/how-we-work/working-with-ai)).
- **`aladawy-shop` has no CI at all.** It has tests and it has a `test` script;
  nothing runs them on a push. Its gates are yours to run by hand until that is
  fixed.
- **`aladawy-links` has no CI, on purpose.** Its workflow was removed because
  a page that changes a few times a year was not worth the pipeline; run its
  five gates, then `vercel deploy --prod`.
- **`adawy-group-links`' deploy job always skips.** `verify` runs on every push
  and PR, but the repo has no `VERCEL_TOKEN` secret, so production ships by
  hand ([deployment](/how-we-work/deployment#client-projects-deploy-the-same-way)).
- **`jumeira` splits its contract across five files.** `AGENTS.md` is the entry
  point, and `SECTIONS.md`, `CHROME.md`, `FILMS.md` and `PRODUCT.md` carry what
  is scoped to one place (plus `masters/README.md` for the film masters). Read the one that covers what you are touching
  ([why](/how-we-work/working-with-ai#every-repo-carries-an-agentsmd)).
- **The CI job is not called the same thing everywhere** — `verify` here and in
  `aladawy-pack` and `adawy-group-links`, `Checks` in `jumeira`, and a reusable
  `verify.yml` called by two workflows in `zad-alhkoul`. Read the repo's own workflow before quoting a job name in a
  PR.

<Callout type="info">
  These gaps are the current state, not the target. The target is the left-hand
  column of that table filled in everywhere it applies. Closing one is a
  legitimate Maintenance card ([the board](/how-we-work/the-board)).
</Callout>

## What is deliberately *not* a repo

- **No per-brand repositories for Group brand sites.** Adawy Studio and Adawy
  Pack are apps inside `adawy-platform`, not repos of their own. Splitting them
  would recreate the ×38 maintenance problem the monorepo exists to solve.
- **No backend repository yet.** The Phase-2 NestJS service joins the platform
  monorepo as `apps/api` when it is built ([ADR-009](/architecture/adrs)).
- **No shared package repository.** `@adawy/*` are workspace packages inside
  the monorepo, published nowhere and versioned with the apps that use them.
- **No shared client-project package.** The repeated patterns —
  `lib/enquiry.ts`, the content/messages split, the publishing switch — are
  documented here rather than packaged, because a client's deliverable has to
  be complete on its own ([ADR-010](/architecture/adrs)).
