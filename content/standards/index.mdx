## How to read a standard here

**Every rule states its *why*.** That is house style, not decoration: a rule
without a reason cannot be argued with, cannot be applied to a case its author
did not foresee, and cannot be retired when its reason expires. **A rule
without a reason is a candidate for deletion** — including the ones already
written.

**Most of these rules are not advice.** Where a rule can be machine-checked it
is, and the build tells you rather than a reviewer
([ADR-011](/architecture/adrs)). When a page says something is an error, it
means the build fails.
