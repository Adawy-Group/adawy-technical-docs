# Standards

Conventions that keep our codebases consistent so anyone can work in any repo:

- **[Coding Conventions](/standards/coding)** — TypeScript style, naming,
  comments, and the lint rules that fail the build.
- **[Project Structure](/standards/project-structure)** — how the monorepo and
  the standalone client repos are organized.
- **[i18n & RTL](/standards/i18n-rtl)** — locales, the content split, and the
  traps that only appear in Arabic.
- **[Testing](/standards/testing)** — what we test, what we deliberately do
  not, and the four gates that block a merge.
- **[Security Basics](/standards/security)** — the non-negotiables.
- **[UI & Brand Standards](/standards/ui)** — design system and component rules.

Every standard here states **why it exists**. A rule without a reason is a
candidate for deletion.
