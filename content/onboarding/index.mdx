## The week

Each step has a time budget and a **done when** — an observable outcome, so you
never have to ask whether you are behind.

<Steps>

### Day 1 — accounts, machine, context

Work the [Day-1 Checklist](/onboarding/day-one) top to bottom: access requested
in one message, the toolchain installed, and the four orientation reads.

**Done when:** `npm run build` passes in your clone of this handbook, and you
can explain what the department builds and why it exists inside a manufacturing
group.

### Day 2 — the code

[The Repository Map](/repositories) — eight repos in two worlds, and the
`Adawy` / `Aladawy` naming trap. Then [Tech Stack & Why](/architecture/tech-stack)
and the [ADRs](/architecture/adrs) (the index table first; read the ones that
touch your work in full).

Get [`apps/group`](/repositories/adawy-platform) running locally and walk the
`@adawy/*` packages to see where the engineering actually lives.

**Done when:** the platform monorepo runs on your machine at `/en`, and you can
say which repo a given piece of work belongs in without looking it up.

### Day 3 — the workflow, watched live

[The Board & Labels](/how-we-work/the-board),
[Git Workflow](/how-we-work/git-workflow) and
[Definition of Done](/how-we-work/definition-of-done). Then watch the board for
a day: how cards move, how PRs link to cards, how the WIP limit behaves.

**Done when:** you can read a card's colours and tell what kind of work it is,
who owns it and what stage it is at, without reading the description.

### Day 4 — standards, and pick a card

Read [Standards](/standards) — [i18n & RTL](/standards/i18n-rtl) and
[Testing](/standards/testing) are the two that decide whether your first PR
passes CI, and [Design Patterns](/architecture/patterns) is what makes an
unfamiliar repo readable.

Pick your first card from *This Week*.

**Done when:** a card is assigned to you and you can state, in one sentence,
what "done" means for it.

### Day 5 — ship it

Take the card through the whole loop: branch → gates run locally → PR linked to
the card → preview → review → merge → **verified live** → Done.
[Your First PR](/onboarding/first-pr) walks each step.

**Done when:** the change is live and you have opened it yourself. Merged is
not shipped.

</Steps>
