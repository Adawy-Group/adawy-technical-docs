# Your First Week

The goal of week one: **understand the why, learn the how, and merge your
first PR.** Asking questions is expected — but check this handbook first, and
when the handbook fails you, fix it.

## Suggested plan

**Day 1 — Setup & context.**
The [Day-1 Checklist](/onboarding/day-one). Accounts, machine, and the three
context reads (About, How We Work, Architecture).

**Day 2 — The code.** *(~5 hours)*
Read [The Repository Map](/repositories) — eight repos in two worlds, and the
`Adawy` / `Aladawy` naming trap that catches everyone once *(~10 min)*. Then
[Tech Stack & Why](/architecture/tech-stack) *(~15 min)* and the
[ADRs](/architecture/adrs) — the index table, then the records that touch your
work *(~30 min)*. They are the fastest way to absorb how this team thinks.

Then [Design Patterns](/architecture/patterns) *(~20 min)*: seven shapes that
recur in every repo, which is what makes the next unfamiliar codebase readable
in an afternoon rather than a week.

Finally, explore the platform monorepo *(the rest of the day)*: run
`apps/group` locally, and walk the `@adawy/*` packages — `ui`, `design`,
`motion`, `i18n`, `site`, `holding`, and the two config packages that enforce
the rules — to see where the engineering actually lives.

**Day 3 — The workflow, hands on.** *(~2 hours of reading, then watch)*
Read [The Board & Labels](/how-we-work/the-board),
[Git Workflow](/how-we-work/git-workflow), and
[Definition of Done](/how-we-work/definition-of-done). Watch the board for a
day: see how cards move, how PRs link to cards, how the WIP limit behaves.

**Day 4 — Standards + first card.** *(~3 hours)*
Read all of [Standards](/standards) — [i18n & RTL](/standards/i18n-rtl) and
[Testing](/standards/testing) are the two that decide whether your first PR
passes CI. Pick your first card from *This Week*
(the lead will have flagged a good starter — typically a small Content or
Maintenance card, or a fix to this handbook).

**Day 5 — Ship it.**
Take the card through the full cycle: branch → gates run locally → PR linked to
the card → preview → review → merge → **verified on the live URL** → Done. That
full loop, not the size of the change, is the point — see
[Your First PR](/onboarding/first-pr).

## People to talk to this week

- **The CTO** — 30 minutes on the roadmap and where you fit.
- **The designer** — how design hands off to development, where the Figma
  source of truth lives, how design tokens map to code.

Both are named on the [Team Map](/about/team), and
[Who to Ask](/help/who-to-ask) is organized by problem rather than by role.

## What a good question looks like here

Asking is expected. Two habits make it cheap for everyone:

- **Search the handbook first** — ⌘K / Ctrl-K, from any page. If the answer was
  here and you missed it, that is a finding about the page, and worth saying.
- **When the handbook fails you, fix it in the same week.** A question you had
  to ask twice is a page that needs writing, and you are the only person who
  can still see what was missing.
