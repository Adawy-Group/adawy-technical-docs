# Day-1 Checklist

Work through this top to bottom. **Every access item is granted by the CTO**
([who to ask](/help/who-to-ask)) — request them all in one message rather than
one at a time, because each one is a round trip.

<Callout type="info">
  Ticks below are saved in your own browser and go nowhere else. They are a
  bookmark for a day that spans several sittings, not a report anyone reads.
</Callout>

## Accounts & access

<Checklist id="day-one-access">
  <Task>**GitHub** — an invitation to the `Adawy-Group` organization, and an SSH key on your own account ([why](/onboarding/environment#prerequisites))</Task>
  <Task>**Trello** — the department board. Read [The Board & Labels](/how-we-work/the-board) before you open it</Task>
  <Task>**Figma** — the Adawy design files and the design system</Task>
  <Task>**Vercel** — team access, so you can see preview and production deployments</Task>
  <Task>**Notion** — the company-wide documentation workspace. Engineering's source of truth is this handbook, not Notion ([ADR-007](/architecture/adrs))</Task>
  <Task>**The team password manager** — the only place credentials live, [never chat or git](/standards/security)</Task>
  <Task>**Company email and the requests channel** — where work reaches the department ([how](/how-we-work/communication))</Task>
</Checklist>

## Machine setup

<Checklist id="day-one-machine">
  <Task>**Node.js 22** through `fnm` or `nvm` — not a system package</Task>
  <Task>**pnpm** via `corepack enable`. Every repo except this handbook uses it</Task>
  <Task>**Git**, configured with your name and work email</Task>
  <Task>**The Vercel CLI**, then `vercel login`</Task>
  <Task>**An editor** with TypeScript, ESLint and Prettier integration</Task>
  <Task>Clone this handbook and get `npm run build` passing — that is the proof your toolchain is right ([full setup](/onboarding/environment))</Task>
</Checklist>

## Context — read in this order

Roughly two hours, and it is the difference between following instructions and
understanding them.

<Checklist id="day-one-reading">
  <Task>[About Adawy Group](/about) — what the business is, and why software exists inside a manufacturing group *(~10 min)*</Task>
  <Task>[The Repository Map](/repositories) — the eight repos, the two worlds, and the `Adawy` / `Aladawy` naming trap that catches everyone once. **Read this before you clone anything second** *(~10 min)*</Task>
  <Task>[How We Work](/how-we-work) — the workflow you will live in from today *(~15 min)*</Task>
  <Task>[Products & Architecture](/architecture) — what we are building and why *(~20 min)*</Task>
  <Task>[Decision Records](/architecture/adrs) — at minimum the index table. The ADRs are the fastest way to absorb how this team thinks *(~30 min for all eleven)*</Task>
</Checklist>

**Done when:** you can run at least one project locally, and explain to a
friend what Adawy Group does and what this department builds for it.
