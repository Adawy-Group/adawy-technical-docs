## Guides this section still owes

Not written yet, in rough order of how often the absence is felt. **Solving
something tricky twice means it belongs here** — write the guide in the PR that
solves it the second time.

- **Adding a component to `@adawy/ui`.** "Fixes flow upstream to `packages/`"
  is stated on four pages and has no procedure behind it.
- **Reading a failing CI run** beyond the summary table on
  [Your First PR](/onboarding/first-pr#when-a-check-goes-red).
- **Adding a locale**, end to end — routing, the message tree, `hreflang`, the
  parity gate, the sitemap.
- **Creating and configuring a Vercel project** — root directory, environment
  variables, domain. Two of its failure modes are already in
  [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
- **The asset bake pipeline.** `assets:bake`, `og:bake`, `pattern:bake` and
  friends are named on six pages and taught on none.
- **Incident response and rollback.** "Production is broken" currently routes
  to the CTO with no runbook behind it.
- **Dependency upgrades**, including the pins that are load-bearing.
- **Data fetching and caching on the platform** — deliberately unwritten until
  a repo actually practices it, because a standard nobody follows is worse than
  no standard.
